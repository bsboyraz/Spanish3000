# Data Sources & Attribution

## Spanish frequency ordering

The vocabulary ordering is derived from the lemma-oriented Spanish frequency data in:

- doozan/spanish_data — https://github.com/doozan/spanish_data
- Hermit Dave/FrequencyWords — https://github.com/hermitdave/FrequencyWords

The upstream repository credits FrequencyWords under CC-BY-SA. This project preserves attribution and distributes the derived vocabulary data with ShareAlike-compatible attribution requirements.

## Turkish learning glosses

Turkish meanings in this project are short study glosses prepared specifically for Spanish 3000. They are intentionally concise and do not represent every dictionary sense of each Spanish lemma.

## CEFR labels

A1/A2/B1/B2 labels used by this application are internal learning bands based primarily on list position and are not an official Instituto Cervantes, DELE, Oxford, RAE, or CEFR vocabulary classification.
